# Agreement with manually marked 2D image angles

This sheet records a small engineering check of Bearing's projected image angle. It does not establish medical accuracy or results for older adults or veterans. Report actual participants and attempts separately.

The app saves no images or video. Any reference photos used in testing must be collected separately with adult volunteers' consent. Get a parent or teacher's approval for the testing plan when students are minors. Keep reference files separate from app storage and follow the consent agreement for deletion.

## Procedure

1. Record the phone model, iOS version, lighting, placement, and final Parameters.swift values.
2. Place the phone upright at chest height. Mark one comfortable position facing the front camera.
3. Give each volunteer an anonymous code. Do not add names or health details to the log.
4. Explain the arm raise and safety text. The volunteer can stop at any time.
5. Record three attempts for each arm as comfortable. Do not ask anyone to move farther for the app.
6. Copy each counted attempt's angle from the recording review before continuing. Log not counted attempts and their reasons too.
7. Mark the same-side shoulder, hip, and elbow in a reference image without the app skeleton visible. Compute the angle between shoulder-to-hip and shoulder-to-elbow in pixels.
8. Match the reference image to the app's peak sample if your test setup supports that. The app reports the median of five smoothed samples, not a single raw photo. If using a separate camera or a different frame, explicitly report the viewpoint, timing, and smoothing mismatch. Do not describe that as an exact same-frame comparison.
9. Keep measurement agreement separate from the proportion of attempts counted. A not counted attempt has no angle error to average.
10. Report the number of volunteers, total attempts, counted attempts, reasons, mean signed difference, mean absolute difference, standard deviation, minimum, and maximum difference.

## Attempt log

All angles and differences use degrees. Signed difference is app angle minus reference angle. Absolute difference is the size of that difference without its sign. For standard deviation, state whether you used sample or population standard deviation.

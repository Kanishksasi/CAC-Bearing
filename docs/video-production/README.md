Prepared October 25, 2026 for Kanishk Sasikumar, Niveth, and Muhammad. Credit the team jointly. Speaking roles are balanced: Kanishk 57 seconds, Niveth 56 seconds, Muhammad 57 seconds. No individual authorship card is required by this plan.

The plan is a 2:50 narrated app demonstration, with 334 scripted words and room for pauses. It is a video built from real app clips, a few stills, concise text, and your own voices. It is not a twelve-slide lecture.

## 1. Gather material before opening the timeline

Make a folder named Bearing Video. Inside, make Footage, Voice, Stills, and Exports folders.

Capture these short clips using the filenames in timeline.csv:

- Home and the main navigation.
- All routines and custom check-in choices.
- Preparation, support wording, and one real arm-raise attempt.
- Start when ready and a genuine countdown. Keep enough footage to show what actually happens.
- My plan, a weekly goal, and local reminder controls.
- English/Spanish, voice-speed settings, and the privacy screen.
- A readable part of the real code and the actual test result, or a clearly labeled text card reporting the verified count.
- The synthetic report overview, ordered attempts, baseline records, and notes.

Use the app version you will submit. Re-record screens that have changed. Do not substitute the simulator's black camera view for a working camera demonstration.

Turn on Do Not Disturb before recording. Hide contact suggestions and notifications. Use a clean demo store, no actual medical history, and only consenting people. Public-video consent is separate from app camera permission.

For camera footage, prop the phone steadily, use even lighting, and perform only a familiar, comfortable arm movement. A screen recording can capture the app while a second device shows the physical setup. Do not stage balance or sit-to-stand without the support and person required by the app. Do not force movement to make a software recording count.

Show one of the three arm attempts in the edited video and label that fact. Do not imply that one attempt produced a complete three-attempt session. You can cut out waiting, but show that the footage was shortened.

If real-device capture does not work reliably, show the actual issue and label synthetic results. Do not edit a simulated success into a false end-to-end demonstration.

## 2. Use a restrained visual style

- Format: horizontal 16:9, preferably 1920 by 1080.
- Background: ivory #F6F5EF, or plain white if the account makes custom colors awkward.
- Headline: dark green #203C32.
- Accent: forest #285442.
- Secondary text: #52645B.
- Soft card: #E6EDE4.
- Font: one clean sans-serif such as Arial. Match weights and spacing across scenes.
- Titles: about 56 to 64 pixels in a 1080p frame; support text about 34 to 40. Adjust by exported readability.
- Captions: roughly 30 to 36 pixels, at most two lines, high contrast.
- Keep text away from the frame edges and from the app's buttons, result disclaimer, and captions.
- Use one headline and at most two short callouts. Narration and captions carry the longer explanation.
- Keep portrait recordings in proportion. Never stretch the phone screen across the landscape frame.
- Show a full screen once for navigation. For details, crop or enlarge the relevant area so a viewer can read it.
- Use mostly straight cuts. Use only a short dissolve when it adds clarity. Avoid spinning text, zoom transitions, stock doctors, and decorative medical imagery.

No background music is the simplest choice. If you add music, use a license that covers the public upload and keep it much quieter than the voices.

## 3. Create the WeVideo project

These instructions use the browser Full-featured editor. Menu wording can vary with school accounts and editor versions.

1. Sign in to your approved WeVideo account.
2. Create a video project/edit and select Full-featured editing when offered.
3. Name it Bearing CAC 2026 - Final Demo.
4. If you are in Storyboard or Simplified mode, use the project menu to switch or convert to Full-featured. Those modes limit separate narration handling; the Full-featured editor supports multiple video/audio tracks. [Editing modes](https://support.wevideo.com/hc/en-us/articles/215518407-What-Are-the-WeVideo-Editing-Modes)
5. Open the editor menu, choose Change format when available, and select Horizontal, 16:9. [Format guidance](https://support.wevideo.com/hc/en-us/articles/6983927751579-How-do-I-share-my-finished-export)
6. Upload a short clip and make a ten-second test export before the main edit. Confirm resolution, download permission, audio, and whether the school license adds restrictions. See export guidance below.
7. Return to the edit and remove the test material.

## 4. Import and organize clips

8. Select +Add media or the upload/import entry in your version. Upload the captured MP4/MOV files, still PNGs, and any prerecorded narration.
9. Find them in Project bin or My Media. Keep filenames numbered by scene.
10. Review every clip before adding it. Reject clips with notifications, private information, unstable framing, or text too small to read.
11. For PDF content, use screen capture of a readable section or page images. Do not assume the editor imports PDF pages directly.
12. The assets folder contains existing stills and a synthetic PDF. The synthetic screenshot filenames are not themselves visible disclosure. Add the on-screen label described below whenever using them.

## 5. Build the visual timeline

13. Use the lowest video track for a solid background, another video track for app footage/stills, a Text track for overlays, and an Audio track for narration. Add a separate music track only if needed.
14. Drag scene 1 onto the timeline at zero. Add the remaining clips in the order in timeline.csv.
15. Drag clip edges to set durations. Use the time ruler and zoom the timeline for precise boundaries.
16. If a clip contains more material than needed, put the playhead at the cut, select the clip, and use the scissors/split control. Delete the unwanted part.
17. Close unintended gaps. The official full-featured editor supports dragging, trimming, splitting, transitions, and Close Gaps. [Timeline editing](https://support.wevideo.com/hc/en-us/articles/7092083436571-How-can-I-edit-clips-in-the-Full-Featured-Editor)
18. Select each portrait clip, open Edit/Transform or the available positioning controls, and scale it proportionally inside the frame. Leave simple side space for a short callout.
19. Use a closer crop for result numbers and report sections. Do not crop out context that changes the meaning.
20. Preserve a natural sequence: choose → prepare → arm start → move → review → share. Do not reorder a result to imply a recording succeeded when it did not.
21. Follow the storyboard's 2:50 timing as a draft. Adjust scene lengths to the actual speech and keep the final runtime below three minutes.

## 6. Add titles and persistent labels

22. Open Text. Choose a plain static text style or a restrained title template.
23. Drag it onto the Text track above the relevant footage. Double-click it or choose Edit, then change the wording, font, color, size, and placement. [Text/title controls](https://support.wevideo.com/hc/en-us/articles/7233830238107-What-are-motion-titles-and-how-can-I-use-them)
24. Use the storyboard's short headline, not the entire narration paragraph.
25. Extend each title clip to cover the correct scene. Watch that it does not accidentally extend into the next scene.
26. Put all three names on the opening and closing cards. Use the spellings and names you will use in the submission.
27. For scenes 5 and 6, keep Synthetic example, not a real record visible throughout the use of sample results or reports.
28. For scene 4, add One of three attempts shown if only one attempt is visible.
29. For scene 11, keep Future idea, not implemented visible throughout the diagram.
30. On the closing card, add exactly: For personal tracking only. Not medical advice or a diagnosis. Share with your clinician.
31. Do not use HIPAA certified, FDA approved, medically accurate, VA partner, or guarantees health improvement as captions.

## 7. Record each person's narration

32. Assign scenes as follows: Kanishk 1/4/7/10; Niveth 2/5/8/11; Muhammad 3/6/9/12. Team credit remains joint.
33. Rehearse once together. Read the actual words aloud and time them. The script has 334 words across 170 seconds, including planned pauses.
34. Use the same quiet room and microphone if possible. Turn off fans, sit a consistent distance from the microphone, and use headphones.
35. Record one scene at a time. Make two takes, then keep the clearest one. Separate scenes make corrections easy.
36. In the editor, put the playhead at that scene's start. Choose +Add media → Narrate when available, allow microphone access, select Record, then Stop and Save. The recording appears near the playhead and can be positioned on the audio track. [Official voiceover steps](https://support.wevideo.com/hc/en-us/articles/6963256830619-How-do-I-record-a-voiceover)
37. Enable Mute video while recording to avoid recording the app's spoken prompts into your narration.
38. If your school interface exposes a microphone/Narrate button instead, use that recorder. You can also record WAV/M4A files outside WeVideo and upload them.
39. Name or organize the chosen clips 01-voice through 12-voice. Place each one under its matching scene, with no overlap between speakers.
40. Trim breaths only when distracting. Leave a short natural pause between ideas.
41. If a paragraph runs long, shorten it or adjust the visuals. Avoid rushing or speeding voices up to fit.
42. Preview transitions between speakers. Match perceived loudness by ear, keep the audio meter out of clipping, and avoid abrupt volume changes.

## 8. Captions and audio

43. Keep the app footage's original audio muted beneath your narration unless deliberately featuring one short app voice cue. Avoid two voices speaking at once.
44. Add captions using the account's subtitle tools, or import a corrected subtitle file if the export workflow supports it. Caption availability can depend on the account.
45. The supplied captions-draft.srt is a timing template, not a finished transcript synchronized to your recordings. Retiming is mandatory after the real voices are placed.
46. Correct names, SwiftUI, clinician, synthetic, and any automatically transcribed app terms.
47. Keep captions to two lines. Check that captions do not cover important app controls, values, disclaimers, or Future idea labels.
48. If caption import is unavailable, use timed text clips with the same short caption groups.

## 9. Final review and export

49. Watch from start to finish with headphones. Then watch muted to check visual meaning and captions.
50. Verify every feature shown as working actually exists. Reminders and user-initiated PDF sharing exist; physician accounts, remote assignments, delivery acknowledgments, completion-aware nudges, and prescribing do not.
51. Check that the app and PDF data are either appropriately consented demonstration data or explicitly labeled synthetic examples.
52. Review the whole export with a teammate who did not edit it. Ask what the app does, who it is for, and which part is only a future idea.
53. Click Export in the upper-right corner. Set a descriptive title, video/MP4 when offered, and 1080p if available. If limited to 720p, check all text at that actual resolution. Choose the frame rate and quality appropriate for the source, typically 30 fps for these captures.
54. Start export and wait for processing. Download the completed file and watch the downloaded file, not only the timeline preview. School administrators may limit download; available resolutions depend on the plan. [Export and download](https://support.wevideo.com/hc/en-us/articles/7275700206363-How-do-I-finish-and-download-my-export)
55. Check for black frames, clipped captions, missing fonts, incorrect synthetic/future labels, and narration cut off at the end.
56. Confirm final duration and keep a local backup named Bearing-CAC-2026-final.mp4.
57. Use an account approved by your school or parent to upload the finished file to the required public video host. Do not submit only a WeVideo editor link. Check playback while signed out.
58. Add the final viewing URL to the submission and complete every required submission question. Confirm that the actual portal shows the completed submission.

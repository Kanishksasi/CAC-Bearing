# Starter assets

These files are existing app previews and a synthetic report. They are not footage of real people receiving care and are not evidence of camera accuracy.

- routine-library.png: supported routine choices. Capture a fresh version if the app screen has changed.
- result-synthetic.png: sample angle result and its explanation.
- manual-count-synthetic.png: a manual entry, not a camera measurement.
- local-plan-synthetic.png: a locally entered plan, not a physician assignment.
- report-synthetic.pdf: six-page example with synthetic values.
- report-synthetic-page-1.png through page-6.png: still pages for WeVideo. Enlarge or crop the relevant section.

Keep Synthetic example, not a real record visible over synthetic results and report footage in the final video. File names do not appear in the video and are not sufficient labels. Do not describe these assets as real-device validation. Real app recordings are still needed for the demonstration plan.

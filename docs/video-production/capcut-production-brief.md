## Visual direction

Use ivory #F6F5EF, ink #203C32, forest #285442, secondary text #52645B, and quiet panels #E6EDE4. Use one clean sans-serif font, generous spacing, at most two short bullets at a time, readable app screens, and a reserved caption area. Preserve portrait phone proportions. Favor original app assets, simple vector diagrams, and restrained motion. Avoid decorative medical graphics, fake app footage, stock dashboards, glitches, spins, and bouncing text.

Use clean cuts within the demo, 0.2 to 0.35 second dissolves only at major scene boundaries, and gentle opening/closing fades. Keep overall timing at exactly 175 seconds after transition overlaps. Hold important screens long enough to read. The demo is one coherent 85-second block, not a montage of unrelated slides.

## Required labels

- Synthetic example, not a real record
- Still preview, not live footage
- Future idea, not implemented
- One of three attempts shown, if the final recording shows only one attempt
- Remaining attempts omitted, if repetitions are cut before completed results
- For personal tracking only. Not medical advice or a diagnosis. Share with your clinician.

Keep the exact disclaimer on displayed result/report material and the closing card. Do not hide it under other overlays. Physician connections, remote assignments, confirmed receipt, and prescriptions are not implemented. Do not show them as functioning screens. No accuracy, health improvement, regulatory approval, partnership, or compliance claims.
